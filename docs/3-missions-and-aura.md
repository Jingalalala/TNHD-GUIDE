# 🎓 Missions, Aura & Rank Progression

## 6. 🎓 Promotion Board

The **Promotion Board** is where you track your career progression.

It contains:
* Available Missions
* Promotion Missions
* Leaderboard
* Promotion Exams
* Rank progression

Your **Aura** is important because it contributes toward your department progression.

---

## 7. ⭐ Available Missions

Available Missions contain regular department tasks that reward Aura upon completion.

> 💡 **Note on Mission Rotation:** Specific daily and weekly tasks rotate automatically and change regularly. The tasks listed below are **sample examples** of missions you may see in your tablet.

### Daily Missions (Sample Examples)
Daily tasks reset every night at midnight. Examples of daily tasks include:
* **Collect Blood (Sample)** – collect blood from 3 patients (`+25 Aura`)
* **On-Duty (Sample)** – remain on duty for 20 minutes (`+30 Aura`)
* **Dispense Medicine (Sample)** – hand out 5 medicines (`+20 Aura`)
* **Revive Patients (Sample)** – revive 3 patients (`+25 Aura`)
* **Treat Patients (Sample)** – provide 5 treatments (`+20 Aura`)

Completing daily tasks grants steady Aura.

### Weekly Missions (Sample Examples)
Weekly tasks reset every Monday. Examples of weekly tasks include:
* **Blood Bank Quota (Sample)** (`+120 Aura`) – Collect blood from 15 patients during the week.
* **Duty Veteran (Sample)** (`+150 Aura`) – Log 150 minutes on duty during the week.
* **Patient Care Quota (Sample)** (`+100 Aura`) – Receive positive ratings from 5 patients.

Weekly tasks generally provide larger Aura rewards.

> ⚠️ **Good Practice:** Do not chase missions at the expense of actual patient care. **Patient safety and proper RP always come first.**

## 8. 📈 Aura & Rank Progression

Aura represents your activity/progression within TNHD.

You can earn Aura through legitimate department activities such as:
* Completing missions
* Completing duty requirements
* Treating patients
* Reviving patients
* Completing department tasks

Your Aura contributes toward your progression and can unlock promotion-related missions or exams.

### Do Not Abuse the System
Never:
* AFK farm duty time
* Fake treatments
* Abuse revive systems
* Complete missions artificially
* Coordinate fake activity for Aura

Aura must be earned through genuine department activity.

---

## 9. 🎓 Promotion Missions

Promotion Missions are different from normal daily/weekly missions. They are connected to your **career progression**.

The tablet displays your current rank and the missions required for progression.

For example:
```text
Dean (Lv 1) – Top earnable rank
```

If you have reached the highest available rank, the system may display:
```text
Top Earnable Rank – Dean
Director is appointed by the department.
```

Follow the rank ladder shown in the tablet rather than assuming that completing normal missions automatically gives you a promotion.

---

## 10. 📝 Promotion Exam

The **Exam** section is used for promotion examinations.

When an exam becomes available:
1. Read the requirements carefully.
2. Make sure you meet the required rank/activity conditions.
3. Complete the examination honestly.
4. Wait for the result.
5. Follow the promotion procedure if successful.

If the tablet says:
```text
★ You have reached the top rank.
No further promotion exams are available.
```
there is currently no further promotion exam available to you.
