TNHD / Roleplay Standards

General EMS Roleplay Guidelines

1. 🎭 Core EMS RP Principles

  • Patient Care Over Everything: Never rush treatments or instantly revive without /me and /do roleplay.
  • Strict EMS Neutrality (Equal Treatment Policy): Treat all players equally regardless of faction, police, or gang status.
  • Fear RP & Value of Life: Your personal safety is top priority. Never run into active gunfights.
  • No Corrupt EMS RP: Never sell or distribute medical supplies or tamper with medical records.

2. 🩺 Medical Roleplay Commands (/me & /do)

Use /me to describe physical actions and /do to query or state environmental/physical facts.

[SAMPLE MEDICAL EXAMINATION]
/me kneels beside patient and checks carotid pulse
/do Is there a strong pulse present?
/do Pulse is weak and rapid at 110 BPM.
/me applies sterile pressure bandage to stop arterial bleeding

4. 📻 Radio Communication & 10-Codes

  • 10-8: On Duty / Available
  • 10-97: Arrived on Scene
  • 10-15: Transporting Patient to Hospital
  • 10-99: Scene Cleared / Available for Next Call
  • 10-33: Emergency Clearance / Officer in Danger

5. 🏥 Patient Transport & Handover

Deploy stretcher, stabilize vitals en-route, transfer patient to ER bed, and log report in tablet before restocking.
