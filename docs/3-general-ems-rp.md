# 🚑 General EMS Roleplay Standards

In addition to following the official TNHD EMS Guidelines, every doctor is expected to maintain immersive, high-quality roleplay (RP) during field and hospital operations.

---

## 1. 🎭 Roleplay Fundamentals

### 1. Patient Care Over Speed
Never rush medical care. Always perform appropriate `/me` and `/do` actions to describe diagnosis and treatments before resolving a call.

### 2. Strict Neutrality
EMS personnel maintain absolute legal and moral neutrality. Treat all citizens equally regardless of whether they are civilians, police officers, or criminal suspects.

### 3. Fear RP & Personal Safety
EMS personnel value their own lives above all else. Never run into active gunfights or hostile scenes without police clearance or armed escort.

---

## 2. 🩺 Medical Roleplay Commands (`/me` & `/do`)

* `/me` – Describes physical **actions** performed by your character.
* `/do` – Asks or states **environmental/physical facts and conditions**.

### Step-by-Step Medical Examination

#### Initial Assessment
```text
/me kneels beside the patient and puts on latex gloves
/do Is the patient conscious or responsive?
```

#### Vitals Check
```text
/me shines a penlight into the patient's eyes to check pupil dilation
/do How do the pupils react to light?
/me places two fingers on the neck to feel for a carotid pulse
/do Is there a strong pulse present?
```

#### Treatment Execution (Gunshot Wounds / Trauma)
```text
/me cuts open the shirt to inspect trauma area
/do What visible injuries or bleeding are present?
/me uses tweezers to extract bullet fragments from the thigh
/me applies sterile gauze and pressure bandage to stop arterial bleeding
/me uses suture kit to stitch the wound closed
```

## 3. 🚨 Emergency Response Codes & Staging

| Code | Definition | Emergency Signals | Driving Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Code 1** | Routine / Non-Urgent | Lights OFF, Sirens OFF | Obey all traffic laws and speed limits. |
| **Code 2** | Urgent Response | Lights ON, Sirens OFF | Moderate speed, siren tap at intersections. |
| **Code 3** | Emergency / Critical | Lights ON, Sirens ON | Emergency speed, slow down at red lights & intersections. |

### Scene Staging Protocol
* **When to Stage:** Active shootings, robbery in progress, gang fights, or hostage situations.
* **Procedure:** Park 1–2 blocks away behind cover. Broadcast: `[Unit Callsign] staging at [Location] awaiting PD scene clear.` Do not enter until PD confirms scene safety.

`[Unit Callsign] to [Dispatch / Recipient] – [Message]`
