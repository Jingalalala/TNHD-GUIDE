# 🏥 THALAINAGARM HEALTH DEPARTMENT

## New Doctor – Tablet & Duty Guidelines

Welcome to the **Thalainagarm Health Department (TNHD)**.

The TNHD Tablet is your main tool for managing duty status, emergency dispatches, assignments, notifications, training, missions, and career progression.

All new doctors are expected to understand and regularly use the following sections.

---

---

## ⭐ Final Message to New Doctors

Being a TNHD doctor is not simply about collecting Aura or reaching the next rank.

Your primary responsibility is to **provide quality medical RP, respond to patients, support your fellow doctors and maintain the reputation of TNHD.**

Use the tablet regularly, stay active while on duty, follow the chain of command and always prioritize **patient care and professional roleplay**.

> 💡 **Welcome to TNHD. Serve with discipline. Treat with care. Lead by example.**
