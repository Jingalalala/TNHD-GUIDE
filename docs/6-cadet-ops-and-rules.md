# 🧑‍⚕️ Cadet Operations & Duty Workflow

Cadet Operations contains specialized tools for senior doctors to train, supervise, and mentor junior medics.

---

## 1. 🧑‍⚕️ Cadet Operations Tools

### Training Module
Senior doctors can host structured training sessions for cadets and junior personnel.
* **Title** (Required, e.g., *Trauma Triage Refresher*)
* **Description** (Optional overview of topics covered)

### Supervised Shift
Senior doctors monitor junior doctors during live duties to:
* Teach correct medical protocols and field diagnosis.
* Guide proper `/me` and `/do` medical roleplay.
* Correct mistakes and evaluate patient handling.

### Reports
Document training results, supervision evaluations, and department field reports professionally.

---

## 2. 👑 TNHD Leadership Tools (Restricted)

Access to leadership modules is restricted to authorized ranks:
* **Roster:** Manage and view all registered department members.
* **Manage Programs:** Organize department clinical initiatives and public health programs.
* **Supervision:** Manage department-wide supervisory assignments.

---

## 3. 🚨 Basic Duty Procedure for New Doctors

### BEFORE DUTY
1. Open the TNHD Tablet.
2. Check your designation and notifications.
3. Verify uniform and gear.
4. Set your status to **On Duty**.
5. Check available assignments and Active Dispatch.

### DURING DUTY
1. Stay available for EMS dispatches.
2. Claim calls promptly and attach to dispatch.
3. Perform realistic medical RP on scene.
4. Transport patients safely to hospital ER when necessary.
5. Complete cases and file reports.

### AFTER DUTY
1. Finish or transfer active cases properly.
2. Ensure no patient is left unattended.
3. Return all EMS gear to storage lockers.
4. Set your status to **Off Duty**.

---

## 4. 🏥 10 New Doctor Golden Rules

1. **Patient First:** Never prioritize Aura, missions, or rewards over patient care.
2. **Do Not Abuse Dispatch:** Only claim calls you intend to handle.
3. **Stay Available:** Be reliable and responsive while marked On Duty.
4. **Follow Chain of Command:** Respect ranks and leadership authority.
5. **Use the Tablet:** It is the primary operational tool of the department.
6. **Earn Aura Legitimately:** Never exploit duty timers or medical mechanics.
7. **Professional RP:** Maintain medical realism and clear communication.
8. **Report Problems:** Report bugs or issues to seniors immediately.
9. **Keep Status Accurate:** Go Off Duty when unavailable.
10. **Represent TNHD:** Serve with discipline, treat with care, and lead by example.
