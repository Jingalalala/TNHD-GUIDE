# 🖥️ Tablet & Duty Guidelines

## 1. 🖥️ Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your main overview.

Use it to quickly check:
* Your current duty status
* Your designation/rank
* Your Aura/experience
* Current department information
* Important updates and activity

Before beginning your shift, open the tablet and make sure your status is correct.

---

## 2. 🚑 Active Dispatch

### What is it?

**Active Dispatch** displays live EMS calls received through the department's dispatch system.

When a new call appears:
1. Check the **Player Name**.
2. Check the **Call ID**.
3. Check the time/location information.
4. **Claim/attach yourself to the call** if you are responding.
5. Use the map/waypoint function to reach the patient.
6. Respond as quickly and safely as possible.
7. Complete the medical situation before leaving the scene.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Do not claim calls that you do not intend to respond to. If you claim a dispatch, you are responsible for handling it unless the situation is transferred to another doctor.

---

## 3. 📋 My Assignments

This section contains cases that have been **directly assigned to you by Dispatch Chiefs or authorized personnel**.

Assignments are different from normal public dispatch calls.

When you receive an assignment:
* Check the assigned case.
* Read the available information.
* Respond to the location.
* Handle the patient/case.
* Complete the assignment once finished.

Senior doctors with appropriate authority may also have the ability to assign cases to other doctors.

---

## 4. 🔔 TNHD Alert / Notifications

Always check **Notification** regularly.

This section contains:
* Department announcements
* Dispatch-related information
* Duty updates
* Aura rewards
* Mission notifications
* Important administrative messages

```text
Example Notification:
[Duty Streak Bonus]
You have served 15 minutes on duty. +30 Aura awarded.
```

Do not ignore department notifications, especially when you are actively on duty.

---

## 5. 👥 Active Units

**Active Units** shows doctors who are currently on duty.

You can see:
* Doctor name
* Aura
* Designation
* Current status

```text
Example Active Unit Display:
🟢 Dcruz Peppe
Aura: 1
Designation: Dean
Status: On Duty
```

This helps doctors and dispatch staff know who is currently available.

> ⛔ **Rule:** If you are not actively working, do not remain marked **On Duty**.
