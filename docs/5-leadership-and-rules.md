# 👑 Leadership, Procedures & Golden Rules

## 12. 👑 TNHD Leadership

Leadership sections are restricted according to rank/permission.

These may include:

### Roster
Used for managing and viewing department members.

### Manage Programs
Used for department programs and activities.

### Supervision
Used by authorized leadership members to manage supervision-related duties.

> ⛔ **Important:** Do not attempt to use leadership functions unless your rank gives you the appropriate authority.

## 🚨 Basic Duty Procedure for New Doctors

Every shift should generally follow this workflow:

### BEFORE DUTY
1. Open the TNHD Tablet.
2. Check your designation.
3. Check your notifications.
4. Check Active Units.
5. Make sure your duty status is correct.
6. Check available assignments/dispatches.

### DURING DUTY
1. Stay available for EMS calls.
2. Monitor **Active Dispatch**.
3. Claim calls you are actually responding to.
4. Respond to patients promptly.
5. Perform proper medical RP.
6. Complete assigned cases.
7. Keep an eye on notifications.
8. Complete legitimate missions when possible.

### AFTER DUTY
1. Finish or transfer active cases properly.
2. Make sure no patient is left unattended.
3. Complete any required documentation.
4. Check your mission progress.
5. End your duty status correctly.
6. Check important notifications before leaving.

## 🏥 New Doctor Golden Rules

1. **Patient First** – Never prioritize Aura, missions or rewards over patient care.
2. **Do Not Abuse Dispatch** – Only claim calls you intend to handle.
3. **Stay Available While On Duty** – If you are marked **On Duty**, other members should be able to rely on you.
4. **Follow the Chain of Command** – Respect Cadets, Doctors, Seniors, Chiefs and Department Leadership according to their roles and authority.
5. **Use the Tablet** – The tablet is not just a menu. It is the department's operational system.
6. **Earn Aura Legitimately** – Never exploit missions, duty timers or medical systems.
7. **Professional RP** – Maintain proper medical roleplay and communicate professionally with patients and other departments.
8. **Report Problems** – If you encounter a bug, dispatch issue, assignment problem or incorrect rank information, report it to your senior/leadership instead of attempting to exploit it.
9. **Keep Your Status Accurate** – Do not remain On Duty when you are actually unavailable.
10. **Represent TNHD** – Every doctor represents the Thalainagarm Health Department while on duty.
