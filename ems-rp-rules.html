TNHD / Roleplay Standards

General EMS Roleplay Standards

1. 🎭 Roleplay Fundamentals

Maintain patient care quality, legal/moral neutrality, and fear for your own safety at all active scenes.

2. 🩺 Medical Commands (/me & /do)

[MEDICAL RP COMMAND EXAMPLES]
/me kneels beside the patient and puts on latex gloves
/do Is the patient conscious or responsive?
/me uses Syringe to draw blood sample from arm
/do What is the blood group test result in the lab?
/me applies sterile bandage and initiates O- blood transfusion
/me applies defibrillator pads and shocks patient

3. 🚨 Emergency Response Codes & Staging

Code Signals Protocol
Code 1Lights OFF, Sirens OFFRoutine / Non-urgent driving
Code 2Lights ON, Sirens OFFUrgent call; siren tap at intersections
Code 3Lights ON, Sirens ONEmergency speed; slow down at red lights
🚨 Staging Protocol: On active shootings or gang fights, stage 1–2 blocks away behind cover until PD announces "Scene Clear".

4. 📻 Radio Status 10-Codes

  • 10-4: Acknowledged / Copy
  • 10-8: On Duty & Available
  • 10-15: Transporting Patient to Hospital
  • 10-33: Emergency Clear Channel / Unit in Danger
  • 10-97: Arrived on Scene
  • 10-99: Scene Cleared / Available
