Cadet Operations & Duty Rules

Cadet Operations and Duty Procedures provide the operational framework for daily EMS shifts, supervisory training, and departmental standards within the Thalainagaram Health Department (TNHD).

1. 🧑‍⚕️ Cadet Operations Tools

Specialized modules designed for senior doctors to train, supervise, and evaluate junior personnel.

🎓 Training Sessions Senior doctors create structured modules (e.g. Trauma Triage Refresher) to teach cadets core protocols before solo duty.
🔍 Supervised Shift Senior doctors monitor junior medics on active field calls to guide proper /me & /do medical roleplay and evaluate care quality.
📄 Reports System Document training performance, supervision evaluations, and field incidents for department records.

2. 👑 Leadership & Supervisory Tools (Restricted)

Restricted to High Command (Chief Medical Superintendent, Assistant Dean, Dean, and Director):

  • Roster Management: Monitor active roster, track member designations, and record certifications.
  • Manage Programs: Organize department-wide health programs, clinical initiatives, and event coverage.
  • Supervision System: Assign and oversee supervisory senior staff across shifts.

3. 🚨 Basic Duty Procedure Workflow

📋 Complete Shift Workflow: Follow this three-stage protocol for every shift on TNHD duty.

1. BEFORE DUTY

  1. Open the TNHD Tablet and review current designation.
  2. Check department notifications and shift announcements.
  3. Equip your official EMS uniform and inspect field gear inventory.
  4. Mark status to ON DUTY on the tablet dashboard.
  5. Verify Active Dispatch and check for pending assigned cases.

2. DURING DUTY

  1. Maintain constant availability for emergency dispatches.
  2. Claim calls promptly and attach your callsign to the dispatch.
  3. Drive safely using Code 1/2/3 emergency signals appropriately.
  4. Perform thorough /me and /do medical roleplay on scene.
  5. Transport critical patients safely to hospital ER when necessary.
  6. Complete medical care, file case reports, and clear the dispatch.

3. AFTER DUTY

  1. Finish or officially hand over any active patient cases.
  2. Ensure no patient is left unattended in ER or field.
  3. Return all issued EMS gear and equipment to storage lockers.
  4. Mark status to OFF DUTY on the tablet dashboard.

4. 🏥 10 New Doctor Golden Rules

⚡ Core Commandments for All Doctors:
  1. Patient First: Never prioritize Aura, missions, or rank progression over patient care. Patient safety always comes first.
  2. Do Not Abuse Dispatch: Only claim dispatches you are actively responding to. Never hog or fake calls.
  3. Stay Available: Be reliable, active, and responsive whenever marked On Duty.
  4. Follow Chain of Command: Respect rank hierarchy and follow instructions given by senior doctors and leadership.
  5. Use the Tablet: Rely on the TNHD Tablet as your primary tool for dispatches, assignments, and updates.
  6. Earn Aura Legitimately: Zero tolerance for AFK duty farming, fake revives, or artificial mission completion.
  7. Professional RP: Maintain medical realism, professional speech, and clear `/me` & `/do` RP at all times.
  8. Report Problems: Report bugs, administrative issues, or disputes to senior staff immediately.
  9. Keep Status Accurate: Immediately go Off Duty whenever taking a break or stepping away.
  10. Represent TNHD: Serve with discipline. Treat with care. Lead by example.

5. 🔐 Equipment Safety & Storage Rules

⛔ Strict Equipment Rules:
  • Return all EMS tools (Tweezers, Suture Kits, AEDs, Medkits, Sedatives, Stretchers) upon going off-duty.
  • Never store medical gear in personal vehicles, personal trunks, or unauthorized places.
  • You are responsible for issued gear — handle it with care and do not distribute tools to unauthorized personnel.
