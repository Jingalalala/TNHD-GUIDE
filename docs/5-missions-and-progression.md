# Missions, Aura & Career Board

The Promotion Board inside the TNHD Tablet tracks your career progression, available daily/weekly missions, promotion tasks, exams, and department leaderboards.

---

## 1. 🎓 Promotion Board Overview
The Promotion Board allows doctors to view their current rank, total earned Aura points, and upcoming rank requirements.

---

## 2. ⭐ Available Missions (Daily & Weekly)
Available missions contain active tasks that reward **Aura Points** upon completion.

> **💡 Dynamic Mission Rotation:** Specific daily (resets midnight) and weekly (resets Monday) tasks change dynamically. The items below are **sample examples** of missions shown in the tablet:
> - **Collect Blood (Sample):** Collect blood from 3 patients (+25 Aura)
> - **On-Duty (Sample):** Remain on duty 20 minutes (+30 Aura)
> - **Dispense Medicine (Sample):** Distribute medicine to patients (+20 Aura)
> - **Revive Patients (Sample):** Revive 3 patients (+25 Aura)

---

## 3. 📈 Aura System & Rules
**Aura** represents your experience, active duty time, and medical contributions within TNHD.

> **⛔ Strict Aura Rules:** Never AFK farm duty time, fake treatments, abuse revive mechanics, or coordinate artificial activity to gain Aura. Aura must be earned through legitimate field duty.

---

## 4. 🔓 Promotion Missions & Unlock System
To advance your medical career and move to higher designations (e.g. Paramedic → Intern → Resident), you must complete **Promotion Missions**.

> **🔑 How Promotion Missions Unlock:**
> Promotion Missions are tied directly to your **Aura Level**. When you don't have enough Aura, missions display as `🔒 Missions Locked`. **Once you earn enough Aura Points from on-duty actions, your Promotion Missions automatically unlock so you can proceed!**

### Step-by-Step Promotion Process:
1. **Earn Aura:** Perform on-duty medical responses, claims, and care to build your Aura level.
2. **Unlock Missions:** Reach required Aura Level (e.g. Paramedic Lv 1 requires **Aura 2** to unlock Intern promotion missions). Once reached, Promotion Missions unlock automatically!
3. **Complete Tasks:** Finish required promotion activity counters & missions.
4. **Take Exam:** Complete the online Promotion Exam via tablet to receive your rank upgrade!

---

## 5. 📝 Promotion Exam & Rank Ladder
When eligible and all promotion missions are completed, access the **Exam** tab in your tablet. Complete the exam honestly to receive official promotion approval from High Command.

*Note: Ranks progress from Paramedic (L0) up to Dean (L4). Director of Medical Services is appointed directly by leadership.*
