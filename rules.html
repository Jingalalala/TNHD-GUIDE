Leadership & Golden Rules

12. 👑 TNHD Leadership

  • Roster: Manage and view department members
  • Manage Programs: Organize department initiatives
  • Supervision: Oversee supervision-related duties
⛔ Restricted: Do not attempt to use leadership functions unless authorized by rank.

🚨 Basic Duty Procedure for New Doctors

BEFORE DUTY

1. Open Tablet → 2. Check designation & Notifications → 3. Check Active Units → 4. Set duty status → 5. Review assignments/dispatch

DURING DUTY

1. Stay available → 2. Claim calls promptly → 3. Perform medical RP → 4. Handle patient care → 5. Monitor notifications

AFTER DUTY

1. Complete active cases → 2. Ensure no patient left unattended → 3. File documentation → 4. End duty status

🏥 New Doctor Golden Rules

  1. Patient First: Never prioritize Aura or rewards over patient care.
  2. Do Not Abuse Dispatch: Only claim calls you intend to handle.
  3. Stay Available: Be reliable while marked On Duty.
  4. Follow Chain of Command: Respect ranks and department leadership.
  5. Use the Tablet: It is the main operational tool.
  6. Earn Aura Legitimately: Never exploit timers or medical systems.
  7. Professional RP: Maintain medical RP and clear communication.
  8. Report Problems: Report bugs or issues to seniors immediately.
  9. Keep Status Accurate: Turn off On Duty when unavailable.
  10. Represent TNHD: Serve with discipline and treat with care.
