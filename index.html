Welcome to the official Thalainagarm Health Department (TNHD) FiveM Guidelines.

💡 Welcome to TNHD: The TNHD Tablet is your main tool for managing duty status, emergency dispatches, assignments, notifications, training, missions, and career progression. All new doctors are expected to understand and regularly use the handbook sections.

⭐ Final Message to New Doctors

Being a TNHD doctor is not simply about collecting Aura or reaching the next rank. Your primary responsibility is to provide quality medical RP, respond to patients, support your fellow doctors and maintain the reputation of TNHD.

Core Oath: Serve with discipline. Treat with care. Lead by example.
