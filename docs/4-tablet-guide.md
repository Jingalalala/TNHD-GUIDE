# 🖥️ TNHD Tablet Operational Manual

The **TNHD Tablet** is your main in-game system for managing duty status, receiving dispatches, handling assignments, and reviewing department notifications.

---

## 1. 🖥️ Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your main operational overview.

Before beginning your shift, open the tablet and verify:
* **Current Duty Status** (On Duty / Off Duty)
* **Designation / Rank** (e.g., Dean, Senior Physician, Paramedic)
* **Aura / Experience Points**
* **Department Announcements & Updates**

---

## 2. 🚑 Active Dispatch

**Active Dispatch** displays live EMS calls received through the department's dispatch system.

When a new emergency dispatch appears:
1. Inspect the **Player Name** and **Call ID**.
2. Check the GPS location and timestamp.
3. **Claim / Attach yourself** to the call if you are responding.
4. Set your GPS waypoint and proceed safely.
5. Provide medical treatment and complete the call before leaving the scene.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Do not claim calls you do not intend to handle. Once attached to a call, you are responsible for it unless transferred to another doctor.

---

## 3. 📋 My Assignments

This section contains cases **assigned directly to you by Dispatch Chiefs or senior doctors**.

Assignments take priority over general public dispatch calls:
* Review case details and patient history.
* Respond immediately to the designated location.
* Complete medical care and file the assignment case report.

---

## 4. 🔔 TNHD Notifications & Alerts

Regularly check your **Notifications** tab for real-time updates:
* Official department announcements
* Dispatch updates & assignment alerts
* Duty streak bonuses (e.g., *Served 15 mins on duty: +30 Aura*)
* Administrative notices
