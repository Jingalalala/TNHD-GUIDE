TNHD / Tablet Operations

Tablet & Duty Guidelines

1. 🖥️ Dashboard

The Dashboard is your main operational overview.

  • Check current duty status
  • Check designation / rank
  • Monitor Aura / experience
  • Review current department updates

2. 🚑 Active Dispatch

Active Dispatch displays live EMS calls received through the department's dispatch system.

[ACTIVE DISPATCH SYSTEM]
1. Check Player Name
2. Check Call ID & GPS Location
3. Claim / Attach to Call
4. Set Waypoint & Respond Promptly
5. Complete Medical Care Before Leaving Scene
⚠️ Important: Do not claim calls that you do not intend to respond to. If you claim a dispatch, you are responsible for handling it unless transferred.

3. 📋 My Assignments

Cases directly assigned to you by Dispatch Chiefs or authorized senior personnel.

  • Review assigned case details
  • Respond to designated coordinates
  • Treat patient and complete case report

4. 🔔 Notifications & Alerts

Regularly check notifications for duty updates, Aura rewards, and announcements.

[TNHD SYSTEM ALERT]
Duty Streak Bonus: Served 15 mins on duty. +30 Aura awarded.
