# 🎓 Missions, Aura & Rank Progression

## 6. Promotion Board

The Promotion Board is where you track your career progression.

It contains:
* Available Missions
* Promotion Missions
* Leaderboard
* Promotion Exams
* Rank progression

Your **Aura** is important because it contributes toward your department progression.

---

## 7. ⭐ Available Missions

Available Missions contain regular activities that allow you to earn Aura.

### Daily Missions
Daily missions reset at midnight. Examples shown in the tablet include:
* **Collect Blood** – collect blood from 3 patients
* **On-Duty** – remain on duty for the required duration
* **Dispense Medicine** – distribute medicines to patients
* **Revive Patients** – revive 3 patients
* **Treat Patients** – provide 5 treatments

Completing these missions gives you Aura.

### Weekly Missions
Weekly missions reset on Monday. Examples include:
* **Blood Bank Quota**
* **Duty Veteran**
* **Patient Care Quota**

Weekly missions generally provide larger Aura rewards.

> ⚠️ **Good Practice:** Do not chase missions at the expense of actual patient care. **Patient safety and proper RP always come first.**

---

## 8. 📈 Aura & Rank Progression

Aura represents your activity/progression within TNHD.

You can earn Aura through legitimate department activities such as:
* Completing missions
* Completing duty requirements
* Treating patients
* Reviving patients
* Completing department tasks

Your Aura contributes toward your progression and can unlock promotion-related missions or exams.

### Do Not Abuse The System
Never:
* AFK farm duty time
* Fake treatments
* Abuse revive systems
* Complete missions artificially
* Coordinate fake activity for Aura

Aura must be earned through genuine department activity.

---

## 9. 🎓 Promotion Missions

Promotion Missions are different from normal daily/weekly missions. They are connected to your **career progression**.

The tablet displays your current rank and the missions required for progression.

```text
Example Display:
Dean (Lv 1) – Top earnable rank

Top Earnable Rank – Dean
Director is appointed by the department.
```

Follow the rank ladder shown in the tablet rather than assuming that completing normal missions automatically gives you a promotion.

---

## 10. 📝 Promotion Exam

The **Exam** section is used for promotion examinations.

When an exam becomes available:
1. Read the requirements carefully.
2. Make sure you meet the required rank/activity conditions.
3. Complete the examination honestly.
4. Wait for the result.
5. Follow the promotion procedure if successful.

If the tablet says: `You have reached the top rank.`, there is currently no further promotion exam available to you.
