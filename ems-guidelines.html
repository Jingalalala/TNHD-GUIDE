TNHD Official EMS Guidelines

1. ✅ What We Should Do (Code of Conduct)

  • Be Respectful: Always stay calm, friendly, and professional when talking to players.
  • On-Duty Conduct: Personnel must maintain character integrity throughout their shift. Disruptive behavior, unattended duty status, or unrealistic actions will not be tolerated.
  • Follow City Rules: EMS follows city rules plus our internal TNHD EMS guidelines.
  • Listen to Seniors: Follow instructions from higher ranks — they are there to help you learn.

2. ❌ What We Should NOT Do

Strict Restrictions:
  • Do not troll, provoke, or intentionally disrupt anyone.
  • Do not argue or create conflict during active scenes.
  • Do not use external or out-of-character (OOC) information.
  • Do not force actions or outcomes on other individuals.
  • Carrying weapons while on duty is strictly prohibited.
  • Do not use EMS status to bypass arrest, death, or police custody procedures.

4. 🏥 Patient Care & Revival Protocol

Always assess patients before taking action. Patients with serious injuries must be transported to the hospital ER.

⚠️ Revival & Blood Bag Requirement: Revivals require visible medical effort. Blood bags (O-, O+, A, B, AB) are strictly required for revivals alongside Defibrillators & Medkits.

5. 🚨 "When the Siren Sings!" — Siren Etiquette

  • Turn on lights and sirens only for real emergencies (Code 3).
  • If not urgent, drive with just lights or no emergency signals.
  • Slow down at intersections — safety over speed!
  • Turn off sirens immediately upon arriving at scene or hospital.

7. 🚑 "From Call to Care" — 7-Step Quick Procedure

1. Wait for Senior Assignment → 2. Radio Confirmation ("EMS-XX attending") → 3. Arrive & Check Safety → 4. Radio Diagnosis → 5. Transport to Hospital → 6. Hospital Treatment → 7. Radio Completion

8. 🔐 Equipment Safety & Storage Rules

Return all EMS equipment when going off-duty. Do not store medical items or blood bags in personal cars — keep them in official EMS storage lockers and Blood Bank units only.
