Cadet Operations & Duty Rules

1. 🧑‍⚕️ Cadet Operations Tools

Specialized tools for senior doctors to train, supervise, and mentor junior medics.

  • Training Module: Host structured training sessions for cadets.
  • Supervised Shift: Monitor junior doctors during live field duty.
  • Reports: Document field evaluations and department records.

2. 👑 Leadership Tools (Restricted)

Roster management, program administration, and supervision tools are restricted to High Command.

3. 🚨 Basic Duty Procedure Workflow

Before Duty: Open Tablet → Check Designation → Uniform Check → Go On Duty → Monitor Dispatch
During Duty: Stay Available → Claim Calls → Perform RP Care → Transport to ER → File Case Report
After Duty: Transfer Active Cases → Return Gear to Storage → Go Off Duty

4. 🏥 10 New Doctor Golden Rules

  1. Patient First: Never prioritize Aura or rewards over patient care.
  2. Do Not Abuse Dispatch: Only claim calls you intend to handle.
  3. Stay Available: Be reliable and responsive while on duty.
  4. Follow Chain of Command: Respect rank hierarchy and senior instructions.
  5. Use the Tablet: Rely on the tablet as your core operational tool.
  6. Earn Aura Legitimately: Zero tolerance for exploits or fake activity.
  7. Professional RP: Maintain medical realism and clear communication.
  8. Report Problems: Report bugs or issues to seniors immediately.
  9. Keep Status Accurate: Go Off Duty when unavailable.
  10. Represent TNHD: Serve with discipline, treat with care, lead by example.
